Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how [Legal organisation name] ("awesomeAIApplications", "we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish and on this website. It applies to every app listed on our apps page. If an app has its own privacy policy, that policy adds to this one.

The short version

  • We don't ask you to create an account.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • Our apps contain no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics.
  • What you create in an app (documents, progress, settings) stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Permissions

An app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it (for example, the camera to scan a document or the microphone to practise speaking). You can deny or withdraw any permission in your device settings; only the related feature stops working.

Payments

Any in-app purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, not your payment details.

Crash reports and support emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance data with us if you have opted in on your device.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security, for a short period.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because our apps keep your data on your device, you can view or delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For any privacy question or request, email [support email].

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
